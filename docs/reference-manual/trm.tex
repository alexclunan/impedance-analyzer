\begin{abstract}
\noindent This manual is the authoritative technical reference for the FPGA-based
multi-frequency impedance analyzer: a digital lock-in amplifier (DLIA) and
low-latency triggering instrument implemented on a Xilinx Zynq-7020 (Trenz
TE0720 SoM) with an AD9122 DAC and AD9467 ADC on a custom carrier board. It
covers the system architecture, the analog and digital signal chains, the
UDP wire protocol, the AXI/SPI register maps, and the physical hardware. It is
one half of the documentation set; the operational and developer \emph{guides}
(build, deploy, GUI, CLI, firmware) live as Markdown under \file{docs/guides/}.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Source of truth.} Every register, struct, port and
scaling constant in this manual is transcribed from current source
(\file{iza\_protocol.h}, \file{iza\_packet.py}, \file{iza\_ctrl.py},
\file{register\_map.py}, \file{iza\_replay.c}, the Verilog, and
\file{full\_design.bd}), not from the historical planning notes archived under
\file{docs/archive/}.
\end{abstract}
